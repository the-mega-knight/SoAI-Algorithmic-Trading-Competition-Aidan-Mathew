================================================================================
FRAMEWORK ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

DataLoader
  └─ Loads OHLCV CSVs from data/ directory
     - Validates columns and timestamps
     - Reports data quality

DataAligner
  └─ Aligns multiple symbols to common timeline
     - Creates union of all timestamps
     - Forward-fills missing prices
     - Returns clean aligned DataFrame

Resampler
  └─ Converts 1-minute candles to other timeframes
     - 5min, 15min, 60min, 1D, 1W, etc.
     - Proper OHLCV aggregation

PerformanceMetrics
  └─ Static utility methods for calculations
     - Returns, volatility, Sharpe ratio
     - Drawdown, turnover, transaction costs
     - CAGR and descriptive statistics

Backtester
  └─ Core backtesting engine
     - Applies strategies to price data
     - Prevents look-ahead bias
     - Accounts for transaction costs
     - Returns metrics and time-series

Plotter
  └─ Visualization functions
     - Equity curves and drawdown
     - Portfolio weights over time
     - Return distributions

================================================================================
AVAILABLE STRATEGIES (example_strategies.py)
================================================================================

1. equal_weight_strategy()
   - Baseline: 12.5% in each of 8 assets
   - Buy-and-hold (0% turnover)
   - Use to benchmark other strategies

2. momentum_strategy()
   - Long 60-minute winners, short losers
   - Assumes continuation of recent trends
   - High turnover expected

3. reversal_strategy()
   - Long 60-minute losers, short winners
   - Assumes mean reversion
   - Opposite of momentum

4. volatility_adjusted_strategy()
   - Risk parity: inverse-volatility weighting
   - Lower-vol assets get higher allocation
   - Reduce drawdowns on volatile assets

5. channel_breakout_strategy()
   - Buy on 20-period breakouts
   - Sell on breakdowns
   - Trend-following approach

6. random_walk_strategy()
   - Random weights (framework validation only)
   - Should underperform after transaction costs

7. composite_strategy()
   - Blends multiple signals (50/30/20)
   - Shows how to combine strategies

8. pairs_trading_strategy()
   - Long worst performer, short best
   - Mean-reversion within pairs
